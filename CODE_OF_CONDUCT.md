# Code of Conduct

Short version: be reasonable.

## What is expected

- Keep it about the work. Criticise the code, not the person who wrote it.
- Assume people are doing their best with the time they have. Most of this is unpaid.
- If someone asks a question you think is obvious, either answer it or scroll past.
- Take the disagreement to the point where a decision gets made, then let it go.

## What is not

- Harassment, slurs, or personal attacks.
- Posting someone's personal information.
- Sexual content or unwanted attention.
- Following someone across issues to keep an argument going.

## Scope

This covers issues, pull requests, discussions and anything else in this repository.

## Reporting

Report it privately to the maintainers rather than in the thread. If it is bad enough to need
reporting, it is bad enough to not want a public argument about it.

Anything reported stays between the reporter and the maintainers. Outcomes range from being asked
to stop, to being blocked from the repository. That call is the maintainers'.
